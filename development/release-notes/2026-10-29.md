# Release Notes: 29 October 2026

## 1. The Chemistry Workbench

In this update the infrastructure has a workbench for chemistry, built of plug-ins ([ADR 0051](../decisions/0051-the-chemistry-workbench.md)). Its first plug-in, *Symmetry and generation*, executes the user's manuscript on chemical structure generation through symmetry breaking, and a note proves what holds and lists what its revision should change.

One can now do six things:
- derive molecules by graph grammars, and see every derivation counted and every symmetry of the rules tested;
- turn the two mirror forms of a stereocentre in three dimensions, with their R and S descriptors;
- find the point group and character table of a molecule, and see whether it may carry a dipole or be chiral;
- watch a symmetric autocatalytic network choose a hand, molecule by molecule, while its law of outcomes stays exactly symmetric;
- read the homology of the manuscript's complexes;
- evaluate Boulatov's tetrahedral field on a finite group, with the Feynman graphs it generates.

Its general mathematics went to morphe (graph rewriting) and physis (point groups, field theories on finite groups, reaction kinetics).

## 2. Fields Known in Part; the Planetary-Science Domain

In this update the infrastructure can read the Moon's magnetic field, and a domain of the planets and moons is founded for it ([ADR 0052](../decisions/0052-fields-known-in-part.md)). Cosmopoiesis, a work in a repository of its own, is the first to use them.

- **The planetary-science domain** is founded, a bridge over astronomy, geophysics and meteorology for the planets and moons as worlds. Its first module, magnetism, gives the Moon's crustal field at any point on or above its surface, from T2015_449, the field mapped from the Kaguya and Lunar Prospector spacecraft. On the low ground of the maria, below the model's sphere, it reads the field at the sphere rather than continuing it downward.
- **Scales finer than the data.** The law of the crust's spectrum is fitted, and the finer structure the model cannot resolve is drawn at random from it, labelled synthetic. Its effective depth of sources comes out at about 6.6 km.
- **The solar wind's field at the Moon.** The interplanetary field measured at L1 reaches the Moon about an hour later, carried at the solar wind's speed, except while the Moon is inside the Earth's magnetic tail.
- **Potential fields in physis.** Fields harmonic outside a sphere, their spectra and random fields with a given spectrum are physis's now (its decision 0010), and calx's Legendre functions hold at every latitude to degree 2700 (its decision 0010). calx also asks the operating system which real-time scheduling classes it would grant.
- **The data library** registers the Moon's crustal field (`moon-magnetic-t2015`, CC BY 4.0) and decompresses a file that arrives gzipped.
- **The globe.** A map of a whole body is now drawn when it is laid on a view that is already close; before, it was never loaded there.

The domain's checks hold the Moon's field to pyshtools at five points on and above the surface to within a ten-billionth of the field, the drawn scales to the law's power, and the solar wind's travel and the magnetic tail to their geometry. It passes them alone, with only the domains and foundations beneath it.
