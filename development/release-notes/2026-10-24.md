# Release Notes: 24 October 2026

## 1. The History Domain and the History Complex

In this update the infrastructure has its first domain of the humanities, history, and an application that draws it ([ADR 0041](../decisions/0041-the-history-domain.md)). The domain is C++ from the start, and has five parts:

- **The record.** A history is recorded as assertions: occasions, the beings present at them, and the ties between beings, each with its sign, sources, confidence and provenance, in the format of a [codebook](../../domains/history/codebook.md). Dates are kept in the Gregorian, Julian or Old Style calendar their sources use.
- **The complex.** Occasions become vertices, the lives of beings edges, and each tie between two of its meetings a 2-cell, whose homology shows the loops where the record is silent and the closed shells of ties.
- **The spin foam.** The signed ties are read as the spin foam of a ℤ₂ gauge theory fitted to the record, each label derived rather than stipulated ([the theory note](../../domains/history/theory/the-history-complex.md)): spins, amplitudes and the Gauss law, Wilson loops that measure a tie's memory, a reconstruction of the signs the record does not attest, and the power of each being over it.
- **Other complexes.** The shells filled by 3-cells, Dowker's complex of who met whom with Atkin's Q-analysis, and persistent homology in time, which morphe gained for it.
- **Joining.** Canons join into one history only where they agree who a being is, by Wikidata item or by name.

Six seed canons, drafted by a language model and not yet checked against their sources, cover Athens (470–322 BC), Florence and Rome, the Scientific Revolution, Europe from Mozart to Schubert, the European alliances before 1914, and Vienna 1890–1938. The data library checks their beings against Wikidata.

The [History Complex](../../applications/history-complex/README.md) shows them in six views. The foam is the history in three dimensions, lives rising through time, cut at any date and opened into its presences. The loom, the network of a moment, the complex, the record and the readings follow. Each canon has a guided tour. A reader's dates follow the historians' convention, Julian before 1582, so that ancient years end where they should. It runs on one's own computer (the Quick Start). The [gallery](../../docs/gallery.md) shows it in twelve pictures, §6, and the whole run counts 2246 checks.
