# Release Notes: 7 November 2026

## 1. The Whole Earth in the Urban Science Workbench

In this update the Urban Science Workbench becomes one three-dimensional GIS from the whole Earth down to a doorstep, and any city in the world stands on it ([ADR 0078](../decisions/0078-the-whole-earth-in-the-urban-workbench.md)). See the [gallery's §16](../../docs/gallery.md).

- **Any place.** Fly to a name, or simply come down anywhere: the place opens with its name, and its buildings, roads, water and open land rise on the real terrain. Nothing has to be prepared for it. Kathmandu opens with about 154,000 buildings. A building's height is recorded where one is known, and otherwise an estimate, drawn fainter and saying so when picked.
- **A page that asks a question first.** Five lenses (People, Movement, Fabric, Risk, Change) replace the list of plug-ins, each layer showing the scales at which it means something. An altitude scale, a compass, a day ribbon drawn from the real Sun and folded legends surround the map. It is pink and purple now, on a plum night or lilac paper, and the Earth's imagery is graded to match.
- **The world's development data, on the map.** The plug-in *The territory* draws where people live, poverty and inequality by economy, a region's people, night lights and built land year by year, urbanisation, settlement spreading from 1985 to 2015, river floods by return period, and any World Bank indicator found by what it means. One can also weigh one's own index of a region. Its *Sources* tool says what was taken from each tool and dataset on the list, what was left aside, and why.
- **A city's flood risk.** The plug-in *Flood risk* draws Freetown's assessment: 1 m flood maps, the 153,435 buildings against them, wards' annual losses and the places the Council records as flooding. Recomputed from its 1,943 modelled events, the annual loss is $808,904.91, the assessment's own figure to the cent. ThinkHazard!'s levels can be asked for a unit anywhere.
- **Reach and use.** Pick a place anywhere, and a wave of minutes runs down the streets from it, on foot or by road. In Melbourne, the walkers' share of each part of the centre is set against their habit, following the clock's date.
- **Areas in dispute.** On the mosaics by economy, a hexagon holding any part of an area in dispute takes no economy's value or name.
- **Beneath it.** The urban-science domain gains `exposure`, `access` and `usage`; the data library reads PMTiles and shapefiles and asks world services on demand; the shared GIS layer gains the Earth beneath a city and the world's tiles, for any application.
