# Release Notes: 30 October 2026

## 1. Contributors, Their Terms, and a Steward

In this update the infrastructure becomes a project of Serendip Commons Society, the user's nonprofit, and records who made it and on what terms ([ADR 0057](../decisions/0057-a-project-of-serendip-commons-society.md)).

- **A steward, not an owner.** The Society approved the project (`SCS/PA/2026/05`) and announced it (`SCS/NTC/2026/02`), both published with OpenTimestamps proofs, and presents it at https://serendip.ngo/science-infrastructures/. It holds no copyright or other intellectual property in the infrastructure and cannot relicense it; the technical decisions stay in this log.
- **Contributors keep their copyright.** Every repository of the infrastructure, the foundations, neuroscience and Cosmopoiesis among them, now carries `CONTRIBUTORS.txt`: the contributors, and the terms on which they contribute. Contributions are made under the same MIT License, to everyone, with a sign-off (`git commit -s`) certifying the Developer Certificate of Origin; there is no contributor agreement. AI assistance is recorded as a tool, never as a contributor. The file also lists the third-party material the repository carries, with its licences.
- **Free to continue.** The licence is unchanged, MIT, its copyright line now "Wanhong Huang and the contributors listed in CONTRIBUTORS.txt". Anyone may use, change, fork and carry on the work, with or without the Society. The README's §8 says so.

## 2. Cosmopoiesis in the Gallery, and a Flight to Where One Already Looks

In this update the gallery shows Cosmopoiesis in full, on its precision console: twenty-two pictures in four parts.

- **One world, its addresses and its instruments.** The console over the Earth and the instant in its scales, the Solar System, Saturn, Mars, Tycho, an address stepped from Fuji's summit, the catalogue's loops and the schedule.
- **Fields.** The Earth's field lines, the Moon's crustal field and its radial part, and fields read over a cube about a point: the Moon's field over Reiner Gamma, the air over Fuji, and the speed of P waves in the Earth beneath the sea east of Sendai.
- **The ground, the sea and what lies beneath.** Where the Moon's heights come from near Hadley Rille, from LOLA's laser tracks to synthetic detail; the Pacific floor off Japan with the water taken away; the sea's temperature and the Kuroshio; and the Earth beneath Japan and the Andes, from the trench to the mantle at true scale.
- **Bodies that move.** Satellites, and the rover's survey of Reiner Gamma with what its mission came to know.
- **Better.** A flight to the place the camera already looks at, such as an address typed for the point in view, no longer leaves the globe's camera undefined and the world undrawn. The arc of a flight is now taken as atan2 of the cross and dot products, exact for small arcs and defined for none.

## 3. The Earth Beneath: CRUST1.0 over PREM, Sections along Geodesics

In this update the infrastructure can say what lies beneath any place on the Earth, from the ground or the sea floor to the centre ([ADR 0055](../decisions/0055-the-earth-beneath.md)).

- **The geophysics domain's first C++ for the solid Earth.** `geophysics::structure` holds CRUST1.0's columns and PREM, the Preliminary Reference Earth Model. PREM's mass is integrated exactly and agrees with the Earth's GM over G to 1.7 × 10⁻⁴. Its gravity is greatest at the core–mantle boundary, 10.69 m/s², and its moment of inertia is 0.3308 M a².
- **The column they compose.** CRUST1.0 to the Moho, PREM below, as a profile to the centre and as physis fields of density and of the speeds of P and S waves. Each field refuses, with its reason, a point in the air or beyond the centre.
- **Sections along geodesics.** A section follows a geodesic of WGS 84 through a place. The section finds where any place lies along and across it, to 10⁻⁹ m.
- **Checked.** PREM is held to ObsPy's tabulation on both sides of each discontinuity, CRUST1.0 to the domain's JavaScript checks, and the sections to geodesy's direct problem; the theory is in a new note, *The Earth beneath a place*.
- **In Cosmopoiesis.** The environment page shows the seismic column beneath the observer, a section through it in three views with Global CMT's earthquakes tracing the sinking plates, and the column to the centre. Its console is redrawn as a precision instrument, with figures that separate into windows of their own (its decisions [0009](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/Cosmopoiesis-Lab/blob/master/development/decisions/0009-a-precision-console-and-figures-that-separate.md) and [0010](https://github.com/huangwanhong-maker/Cosmopoiesis-Lab/blob/master/development/decisions/0010-the-world-beneath-the-ground-and-the-sea.md)).
- **The gallery.** Ten of Cosmopoiesis's pictures are taken again on the new console. Among them are new ones of the Earth's field lines, the Moon's crustal field, and the field about a point, read over a cube above Reiner Gamma.
