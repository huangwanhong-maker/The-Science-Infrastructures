# Release Notes: 6 November 2026

## 1. Physical Computing: the Formalism and the First Simulators

In this update the physical-computing domain begins to compute ([ADR 0076](../decisions/0076-physical-computing.md)). Its formalism is written, and the simulators of three of its fourteen families run: water and interfaces, chemical media and reaction networks, and DNA. Each is held to a closed form or a published result, in 127 checks. The laws they rest on went first to physis (its decision 0022).

- **What a computation is.** A task states what is wanted and its tolerance, and a schedule how it is run. A result is accepted only when it agrees with the intended computation within that tolerance. Its cost in energy is set against the floors of physics (Landauer's bound and the thermodynamic uncertainty relation), and its materials are marked against the EU's critical raw materials.
- **Every simulator is a backend.** Each one implements one interface and is driven by one runtime, so a real device can later be held to the simulator. A new family joins by adding a directory and a backend; nothing written changes. The intermediate language stays optional, for later.
- **Water and interfaces.**
  - **Convection and rotation:** a layer heated from below and its Lorenz truncation; a rotating annulus, cooled inside and warmed outside, breaking into four baroclinic waves, the regular-wave regime of Fultz's and Hide's tanks.
  - **Shaken and walking:** Faraday waves on a shaken layer at their thresholds; droplets walking on their own waves.
  - **Reservoir and maze:** a tank of water as a reservoir that remembers and forgets; a maze solved by flow.
  - **Foam and bubbles:** a foam coarsening, each cell growing or shrinking by its number of sides as von Neumann's law says (zero at 6.11 sides, read from the lattice); logic by bubbles.
  - **An economy in water:** Phillips's hydraulic machine of the economy.
- **Chemical media and programs.**
  - **The BZ reaction** stirred and oscillating at its Hopf point, and in a gel, where a broken wave curls about its free end and keeps turning.
  - **Two coupled cells** that lock.
  - **Reaction networks as programs**, run exactly.
  - **Dissolving electrodes** that synchronise.
- **DNA.** Any reaction network compiled into strand displacement, approaching it as 1/C_max; Qian and Winfree's seesaw gates; and a molecular ecosystem of predator and prey that oscillates beyond its Hopf point and invades as a front.
- **Gallery.** Sixteen figures in the gallery's §15, from the record `research/experiments/2026-11-06-first-physical-substrates`.
- **Next.** The other eleven families are planned, among them analogue electronics, p-bits and networks that learn.

## 3. Agents in the City: Walkers, Households, Owners and Occupiers

In this update the urban-science domain gains agent-based models, built on nomos's agents and tyche's decisions to the design of ADR 0071, each with several tasks in a case study and the walkers drawn in the Urban Science Workbench ([ADR 0075](../decisions/0075-agents-in-the-city.md)).

- **Walkers** (`movement`) cross the Hoddle Grid one by one.
  - **Calibration.** Their way is chosen by the same logit over walks as the calibrated flow, so their mean is that flow exactly: R² 0.720 against the counters over a week of 3.16 million trips.
  - **Walking.** They slow in crowds, queue behind full streets and wait at signals.
  - **Tasks.** A street closed, a festival's crowd, and a street's regime decided online by POMCP on the walkers themselves.
  - **Caveat.** The record finds that the decision is degenerate for that street: closure wins whatever the crowd.
- **Households** (`housing`) choose the centre's dwellings, and rents clear the market.
  - **Calibration.** Homes Victoria's rents of new lettings to a log error of 0.105, and the 2021 Census's sorting of incomes.
  - **Supply shocks.** Every approved tower lowers the centre's rents by about 7 % while it fills, and adds 40 % more low-income households.
  - **Sorting.** The city's sorting is what prices alone give, well short of Schelling's limit.
- **Owners and occupiers** (`occupancy`) carry the Hoddle Grid's buildings through their uses, 2002–2024, on physis's nested chain.
  - **Vacancy.** The model needed a vacancy channel that the record demanded.
  - **Planning.** A planner of incentives for vacant space reaches 97–98 % of its bound under a budget, against 74–81 % for simple rules.
  - **Caveat.** Vacancy's jump after 2020 is not reproduced, and the record says so.
- **The workbench's *Human flow*** draws every walker on the 3-D map, moving with the clock, beside the counters' readings. A street can be closed, made one-way or phased, an event's crowd brought from the City's permits, and POMCP asked for the regime.
- **Data.** Eight new manifests (CLUE's dwellings, blocks and floor space; building permits; planning zones; Homes Victoria's rents; the 2021 Census and its areas) and two products, all CC BY 4.0.
- **Gallery.** 39 new pictures in the gallery's §14.
- **Simulated stays simulated.** Every simulated history is marked so, and never drawn as observed.

## 4. Physical Computing, a Domain Planned

In this update the infrastructure gains a plan for computing with matter itself: a new domain, physical-computing, that holds analogue circuits, chemical media, tanks of water, memristors, networks of oscillators and living cultures as computers ([ADR 0076](../decisions/0076-physical-computing.md)). Nothing is computed yet; what exists is the design that every simulator to come will follow.

- **What a physical computer is.** The domain's theory note defines one precisely: a square that commutes within a tolerance, with an encoding and a readout of a kind fixed in advance. It sets out four ways of computing with matter, what carries over from a substrate to the system it stands for, and how the energy and materials of a verified result are counted.
- **A pipeline after TVM.** Tasks, the compiler, the schedule, the runtime and the backends each have a directory and a plan; an intermediate language is left optional, for later. Every family's first backend will be a simulator, the reference that any real device is held to.
- **Fourteen families of substrates.** They cover every candidate discussed, from analogue electronics, reaction networks and DNA to rotating tanks of water, ecological microcosms, quantum reservoirs and robot swarms. Each family's plan names the models of its simulators and the closed forms they must reproduce before they are trusted.
- **Next.** Simulators for the most rooted families first: analogue electronics, chemical networks, p-bits and networks that learn.
