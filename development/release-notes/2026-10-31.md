# Release Notes: 31 October 2026

## 1. Bodies with Joints: a Rabbit on the Moon, a Fish in the Ocean, a Dog on the Ground

In this update the robotics domain has three robots, each a body with joints in the environment it was built for, and the world presumes what nothing has measured about the places they move in ([ADR 0058](../decisions/0058-a-body-with-joints.md), [ADR 0059](../decisions/0059-robots-in-their-environments.md)).

- **A rabbit on the Moon.** A hopping robot of 1.69 kg as built: six links, five geared motors with their torque–speed lines and copper loss, an Achilles tendon and compliant feet. Its controller stands, hops in place, hops forward at the speed asked and stops, and refuses what the body cannot do: a hop on the Earth is refused, the ankle too weak. It is moved by three methods of one motion (the discrete action's variational integrator, Runge–Kutta, semi-implicit Euler), which agree to millimetres and differ in what they keep: energy residuals of 0.021, 0.115 and 0.216 J. The model found a flaw of the build: sitting, the ankle's motor holds the tendon with 12 W of copper loss.
- **A fish in the ocean.** A head and four driven links of 1.85 kg, 0.68 m long, swimming by a travelling wave in water modelled strip by strip: the water's added mass in its Lagrangian, its drag a force, the current carrying it. It swims by drag at a Strouhal number near 2.5, where real fish swim at 0.2–0.4; strip theory leaves out the momentum a tail sheds into its wake, and the domain says so.
- **A dog on the Earth's ground.** A four-legged robot of 10.2 kg that trots on diagonal pairs, its stance pair's force distributed within the friction it believes the ground gives. On the Greenland ice sheet, told μ 0.15, its feet learnt 0.296 where the ice was 0.317.
- **What a world presumes of a place.** Where nothing has measured a property, the domain that names the material gives its law with its spread: planetary science the Moon's regolith (its friction from the angle of internal friction), oceanography the upper ocean's density and current (its first C++), geophysics the Earth's ground by CRUST1.0's classes, ice, sediment or rock, the sea refused. A simulated world stands its robots on a realisation of that law, drawn with the world's seed; their feet read it, and their belief is the presumption conditioned on the readings, calibrated by construction and able to tell when the presumption is wrong.
- **Every field at a point.** Cosmopoiesis shows every field of the world at the point chosen, each component with its unit and provenance, or the reason it has none there; the ground and the water are among them. Its ROBOT page runs the three robots at their sites and draws them in the world at true scale.
- **A flight that lands where it was sent.** The virtual globe's flights now arrive at the latitude flown to on an ellipsoid; on WGS 84 they had landed 42 m off at Greenland's latitudes.
- **The gallery** shows the three robots and what they learn of their ground ([§9.5](../../docs/gallery.md)).

The foundations' parts are physis's planar trees and compliant contact, its stationary random fields read at any point, and its bodies in a fluid with the Euler–Lagrange equations solved for the acceleration (its decisions 0014, 0015 and 0017).
