# Release Notes: 31 October 2026

## 1. Landscapes as Cells: Their Events, Their Composition and Their Spacetime

In this update a district can change, and the infrastructure can say exactly what changes with it ([ADR 0062](../decisions/0062-landscapes-as-cellular-configurations.md)).

- **A district as cells.** The walking network of the Hoddle Grid's middle becomes a regular cell complex: 356 junctions, 399 segments and 37 blocks. Blocks with a dead end inside are left as holes, 12 of them. Its events are rewrites of the cells. A lane between two stores is two refinements (the streets cut where it meets them) and an intervention (the block split), and closing it gives the district back.
- **Structure, use and meaning in one process.** Walkers walk each way along the streets and the blocks warm with the Sun. Two real stores open and shut. A rule comes into force, is carried out as a lane, and takes on a meaning as the lane is used. A recognised lane can no longer be removed. The day runs as one hybrid process with its record, the causal order of its events, and at each hour the events that could happen.
- **The district's spacetime.** A new layer draws the day with time rising above the map. The streets' traffic, slice by slice, shows the day's tide as a pattern of the whole. The stores rise as worldlines, thick while open. The lane's sheet begins in mid-air at the instant it is built and deepens once it is recognised. The events are beads joined by the order in which they read one another.
- **Exactly, beside the map.** The same events, composed from five parts, are solved without simulation:
  - whether the whole runs round its cycles (a meaning adopted through use always does);
  - which coarser descriptions forget something, the two states each confuses, and for how long each stays within 10%;
  - how promotions and permits interact on an outcome;
  - what a record says of how much the stores answer each other.
- **Buildings, in architecture.** A building picked on the map is filled with rooms, and its heat is followed through the day under the real Sun. The light the walls and roof absorb mostly goes back to the air, as the sol-air rule says. Its doors, stairs, and merged and parted rooms keep its heat. These are the architecture domain's first modules, which is now a draft.
- **Scales and memory.** The district's own frequencies show how fast it evens out (72 minutes) and along which division. It has flows round its holes that nothing at a junction can start or stop. Leaving the shops out of a description of its streets leaves a memory: those who went in come out later, where they went in.

The foundations' parts: morphe's regular complexes, their rewriting, traces and Möbius inversion (its decision 0008); physis's composition of open systems, lumpability and its bound, jump and piecewise-deterministic processes, exact elimination, Doi–Peliti operators, Hodge Laplacians and the faces of plane graphs (its decision 0018). The theory note `domains/urban-science/theory/landscape-dynamics.md` states the formalism with its proofs.

## 2. The Domains of Life, from the Molecule to the Ecosystem

In this update the life sciences have their place in the order of the domains ([ADR 0061](../decisions/0061-the-domains-of-life.md)). Six domains are founded as planned, beside neuroscience, genomics and evolution, which have code.

- **Molecular science** is the domain of molecules as graphs, shapes and wave functions. It also holds proteins and nucleic acids in space, and reactions between named molecules. Cell biology stands on it, and chemistry and drug discovery will.
- **Cell biology** stands on genomics and molecular science. It treats the cell as a system: gene regulation, signalling and metabolism as networks, membranes, the cell cycle, and the states a cell can be in.
- **Microbiology, physiology and developmental biology** stand above cell biology:
  - microbiology holds the growth, pangenomes, classification, communities, phages and infections of microbes;
  - physiology holds excitable tissues, circulation, breath, homeostasis, immunity and the physiology of plants;
  - developmental biology holds how an organism forms, from gradients, Turing patterns and segmentation clocks to tissues as cell complexes and growth by rewriting.
- **Ecology** stands on geography, climate and evolution, and is the highest domain in the order, at height 5. It holds populations, communities, behaviour, biodiversity, the distributions of species, ecosystems and fisheries.

Each domain's README gives its modules, what it will take from the foundations, the questions it leaves open and its references. The record says what each holds against its neighbours. The README's map of the sciences now links every domain with a directory and counts fifty-seven domains, seventeen of them built. It also marks genomics and evolution as built, which it had not yet done.

## 3. Streets Read as a Sea: the Urban-Science Domain and the Urban Building Workbench

In this update the infrastructure has a domain of streets and a three-dimensional GIS of a city to read them in, from the user's *Street Oceanography* ([ADR 0060](../decisions/0060-streets-as-open-systems.md)).

- **A city in three dimensions.** The Urban Building Workbench draws Melbourne's centre from OpenStreetMap: 15,038 buildings at their mapped heights (paler where a default stands), its ways at their widths, its parks and water, lit by the real Sun of any hour in Melbourne's own time. The buildings can stand as built, lowered, or of glass, so that the streets show between the towers. Its colour is a gentle sea blue, the user's choice.
- **The tide on the streets.** Each pedestrian sensor's street is a channel of water as deep as its walkers are many, one metre for every hundred an hour. The level is the tide the calendars predict, fitted to two years of counts, rising and falling as the clock runs. On a date in the record the water stands at that hour's count, coloured by its surge against the tide; a holiday sinks below its tide marks. The time of high water and the kind of tide (one crest a day or two) colour it too. On an ordinary Wednesday the city's counts came within 1% of its tide.
- **Currents.** Melbourne counts each way at 92 places. Their streets carry walkers each way, as many as are really there at that hour, moving in real time, coral toward the centre and sea blue away; an arrow gives the net current. The morning flood and the evening ebb can be watched.
- **Waves on Swanston Street** (a model): the crowd walking north from Flinders Street, second by second, its blocks and crossings taken from the map and its walkers entering at the counted rate, queuing at red and spilling back.
- **The city's tides and currents**, read together: half the streets reach high water at the evening commute and 41 at midday; 72 of 100 have one crest a day; the counting lines take in about 5,200 more people than they let out by 17:00; the walkers' entropy production, bounded below by the measured edges, peaks at 08:30 on weekdays; and on 29 of 41 footpaths walkers keep left (a sign test, p = 0.006).
- **And beside the map:** the rhythm of a street on the calendars and whose clock it keeps, its response to rain and holidays, the mix of its uses, its opening hours and who they draw, two stores picked on the map as a pathway whose visitors spread along the street, crowds and circulation, and kinds of streets.
- **Architecture** is now a domain of its own (planned), at the user's word; urban science keeps the street and the city, and will stand on it.
- **The data library** reads Melbourne's pedestrian counts each way since 2009, its census of land use, the hourly weather, Victoria's holidays, and OpenStreetMap's holdings, opening hours and city model. The visualization layer gains the city GIS and two sea maps; the gallery shows the workbench ([§10](../../docs/gallery.md)), and so does the README's front page.

The foundations' parts: calx's multitaper spectra and bispectra, and a QR four times faster with the same factors to the bit (its decision 0013); physis's kinematic waves, master equations and reaction–diffusion on graphs (its decision 0016); tyche's spectral inference, harmonic analysis and point patterns (its decision 0005).

## 4. Bodies with Joints: a Rabbit on the Moon, a Fish in the Ocean, a Dog on the Ground

In this update the robotics domain has three robots, each a body with joints in the environment it was built for, and the world presumes what nothing has measured about the places they move in ([ADR 0058](../decisions/0058-a-body-with-joints.md), [ADR 0059](../decisions/0059-robots-in-their-environments.md)).

- **A rabbit on the Moon.** A hopping robot of 1.69 kg as built: six links, five geared motors with their torque–speed lines and copper loss, an Achilles tendon and compliant feet. Its controller stands, hops in place, hops forward at the speed asked and stops, and refuses what the body cannot do: a hop on the Earth is refused, the ankle too weak. It is moved by three methods of one motion (the discrete action's variational integrator, Runge–Kutta, semi-implicit Euler), which agree to millimetres and differ in what they keep: energy residuals of 0.021, 0.115 and 0.216 J. The model found a flaw of the build: sitting, the ankle's motor holds the tendon with 12 W of copper loss.
- **A fish in the ocean.** A head and four driven links of 1.85 kg, 0.68 m long, swimming by a travelling wave in water modelled strip by strip: the water's added mass in its Lagrangian, its drag a force, the current carrying it. It swims by drag at a Strouhal number near 2.5, where real fish swim at 0.2–0.4; strip theory leaves out the momentum a tail sheds into its wake, and the domain says so.
- **A dog on the Earth's ground.** A four-legged robot of 10.2 kg that trots on diagonal pairs, its stance pair's force distributed within the friction it believes the ground gives. On the Greenland ice sheet, told μ 0.15, its feet learnt 0.296 where the ice was 0.317.
- **What a world presumes of a place.** Where nothing has measured a property, the domain that names the material gives its law with its spread: planetary science the Moon's regolith (its friction from the angle of internal friction), oceanography the upper ocean's density and current (its first C++), geophysics the Earth's ground by CRUST1.0's classes, ice, sediment or rock, the sea refused. A simulated world stands its robots on a realisation of that law, drawn with the world's seed; their feet read it, and their belief is the presumption conditioned on the readings, calibrated by construction and able to tell when the presumption is wrong.
- **Every field at a point.** Cosmopoiesis shows every field of the world at the point chosen, each component with its unit and provenance, or the reason it has none there; the ground and the water are among them. Its ROBOT page runs the three robots at their sites and draws them in the world at true scale.
- **A flight that lands where it was sent.** The virtual globe's flights now arrive at the latitude flown to on an ellipsoid; on WGS 84 they had landed 42 m off at Greenland's latitudes.
- **The gallery** shows the three robots and what they learn of their ground ([§9.5](../../docs/gallery.md)).

The foundations' parts are physis's planar trees and compliant contact, its stationary random fields read at any point, and its bodies in a fluid with the Euler–Lagrange equations solved for the acceleration (its decisions 0014, 0015 and 0017).
