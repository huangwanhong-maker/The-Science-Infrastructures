# Release Notes: 28 October 2026

## 1. Instruments and Their Acquisition; the Spacetime of the Earth and the Moon

In this update the domains of time, the Earth and the sky gain their C++, and with them any work can place, address and observe a point of the Solar System ([ADR 0049](../decisions/0049-the-spacetime-of-the-earth-and-the-moon.md), [ADR 0050](../decisions/0050-instruments-and-their-acquisition.md)). Cosmopoiesis, a work in a repository of its own, is the first to compose them.

- **Time and the Earth's orientation.** Metrology computes every time scale after SOFA, with the leap seconds and TDB − TT. It reads the IERS's Earth orientation and evaluates IAU 2006/2000A in full, with GMST of 1982 for SGP4's frame.
- **The figure of the Earth.** Geodesy has the ellipsoids, Karney's geodesics, the transverse Mercator by Krüger's series, UTM, UPS and MGRS, the EGM96 geoid, and now the normal gravity of a level ellipsoid at any height.
- **The bodies.** Astronomy reads JPL's ephemerides and binary PCK files, and turns the bodies by the IAU's models and the Moon by DE440.
- **The navigation bridge** is founded. Its catalogue of the Solar System's frames is one graph, from the barycentre to a place on the Moon and the Galaxy's centre, and its loops measure how far two standards disagree. It propagates satellites by SGP4 in TEME and gives the attitude of vehicles.
- **Addresses.** Any point of the Solar System or the Galaxy can be written in one line: a body or a frame, a chart, coordinates, steps from a local horizon, and an instant. Navigation reads the line with one parse, prints it canonically, and answers it in every frame and chart, each answer reading back to the same point.
- **What instruments read.** Geophysics evaluates the World Magnetic Model 2025, held to its report and to NOAA's test values, with three misprints of the report stated. Meteorology has its first C++, the U.S. Standard Atmosphere 1976 to 86 km.
- **The foundations.** physis gains rotations, moving frames with their holonomies, and the two-body problem; calx gains real-time scheduling (deadlines, servers, elastic degradation, simulation and dispatch) and the Legendre functions of geomagnetism.
- **The data library** builds terrain tiles of the Earth and the Moon on demand, every sample saying where it came from. It also registers Earth orientation, the leap seconds, the geoid, names of places and lunar features, satellites' elements, the geomagnetic model and space weather in real time.
- **The visualization layer** draws virtual globes with a level of detail after Cesium's, flights between bodies, bodies' maps, the Sun's light and the Milky Way.
- **The gallery** shows Cosmopoiesis in nine pictures: the console over the Earth, the instant and the sky, the Solar System, Saturn, Mars, Tycho, an address stepped from Mont Blanc, the catalogue's loops beside the schedule, and the satellites.

The whole run counts 6856 of 6862 checks in thirty-two suites. The six that fail belong to work in progress beside this update, not part of it: the new foundations tyche and nomos, and genomics' grammars. Every domain of this update passes its checks alone.
