### A District's Economy: the Problem

The ground floors of the blocks between Swanston and Russell Streets, Flinders Lane and Bourke Street, in Melbourne's Hoddle Grid, are set as one problem for three studies. Their models of the people differ: none, a fluid with its circulation, and a field theory with an action. Each of the 61 frontages takes a kind and its weekday and weekend hours, and eleven links may be made. The value is the week's gross operating surplus: the margin on what passers spend at open frontages, with catalysis between neighbouring kinds from CLUE's co-location quotients and competition along a street. The people come from the City's counters, read as tides by the urban-science domain. A study supplies only the passers at each frontage and hour; the rest is shared ([the record](README.md)). This is a study of methods on a real place, not a planning proposal.
