### The Walkers of the Hoddle Grid

The district of [the shared problem](../research/experiments/2026-11-05-a-district-economy-the-problem/README.md), between Elizabeth and Russell Streets and between Flinders Street and Lonsdale Street, walked by agents: urban science's new module `movement` ([ADR 0075](../development/decisions/0075-agents-in-the-city.md)). Each walker has an origin and a target and chooses its way junction by junction by the flow study's logit over walks, read as a Markov chain, so that the walkers' mean is that flow exactly. It walks at its own speed, slowed by the crowd ahead of it, waits at red signals and for room at full streets, and may dwell and walk back. The walkers are driven by the flow study's trips, calibrated to the City's counters each way. Four tasks follow: the mean week; Little Bourke Street closed, as the City's permit closed it for the Lunar New Year of 2017; the festival's crowd that day, fitted to the counters; and the street's regime decided every half hour by POMCP planning on the walkers themselves. This is a study of a model on a real place, not a planning proposal ([the record](../research/experiments/2026-11-05-walkers-of-the-hoddle-grid/README.md)).
