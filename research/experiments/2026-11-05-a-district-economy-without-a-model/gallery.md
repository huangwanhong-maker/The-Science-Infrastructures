### A District's Economy without a Model

The first of three studies of [the shared problem](../2026-11-05-a-district-economy-the-problem/README.md): the people come from the data alone. Their passers at each frontage are kriged from the City's counters on tyche's Gaussian conditioning. A plan changes them only by a stated, cross-sectional correlation with the open establishments nearby, with no conservation and no network. The value is then searched as a black box by heuresis's GA, separable CMA-ES on a relaxation, Bayesian optimisation on a random embedding, MAP-Elites, NSGA-II and random search, at equal budgets over five seeds, with a GA started from the plan as built beside them ([the record](README.md)). This is a study of methods on a real place, not a planning proposal.
