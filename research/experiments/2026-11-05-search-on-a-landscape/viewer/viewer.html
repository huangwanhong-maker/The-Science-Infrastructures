Six searches in one landscape, step by step

The (1+1)-ES, CMA-ES, differential evolution, a particle swarm, the cross-entropy method and Bayesian optimisation, each started the same way and run by heuresis, replayed at equal numbers of evaluations. Drag the count or press play; the shading is the landscape, log f − f*, darker where lower.

What each search keeps, and what is drawn

(1+1)-ES
One parent and a step size σ, adapted by the one-fifth success rule. Drawn: its path, the latest offspring, and the circle of radius σ.
CMA-ES
A Gaussian N(m, σ²C) whose covariance learns the shape of the valley. Drawn: the path of its mean, the generation's samples, and its 1-σ and 2-σ ellipses.
Differential evolution
A population that moves by the differences of its own members. Drawn: the population after selection (filled) and the trial points of the generation (rings).
Particle swarm
Particles with velocities, drawn towards their own and their neighbours' best (the Standard PSO 2011). Drawn: the particles and the steps that brought them there.
Cross-entropy method
A Gaussian refitted to the best samples of each generation, smoothed. Drawn: its samples and its 1-σ and 2-σ ellipses.
Bayesian optimisation
A Gaussian-process model of the landscape, sampled where the expected improvement is largest. Drawn: every point it has evaluated, over its model's mean in place of the true landscape. Its budget is 100 evaluations.
