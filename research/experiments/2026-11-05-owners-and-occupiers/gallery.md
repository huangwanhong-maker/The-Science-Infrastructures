### Owners and Occupiers of the Hoddle Grid's Buildings

The 1,535 buildings of Melbourne's Hoddle Grid, 2002–2024, as agents: each building's owner and occupier, whose baseline is the record's own memory, physis's nested chain of uses (exactly, when the modulation is off). Added to it: occupiers leaving with no successor, vacancies let (half of them back into the family they held), and the owner's choice of a use pulled by the neighbours' uses. The model is calibrated to the City's censuses (CLUE) by indirect inference. A planner then spends a yearly budget on vacant space, as a weakly coupled POMDP over the buildings under tyche's Lagrangian bound. This is a study of methods on a real place, not a planning proposal ([the record](../research/experiments/2026-11-05-owners-and-occupiers/README.md); [the module's theory](../domains/urban-science/theory/occupancy.md)).
