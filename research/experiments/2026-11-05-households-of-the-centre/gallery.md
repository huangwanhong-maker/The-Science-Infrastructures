### The Households of the Centre

The City of Melbourne's households as agents among its 11,036 residential buildings, 2002–2025, on urban-science's `housing` ([ADR 0075](../development/decisions/0075-agents-in-the-city.md)). Each quarter, residents leave or consider a move, and newcomers arrive as the demand that the rents reveal. Searchers choose among the dwellings on offer by a logit in rent against the metropolis's, type, distance and neighbours. Each segment's rent moves with its excess demand. The model is calibrated to Homes Victoria's rents of new lettings, CLUE's stock and the 2021 Census, and four tasks are run on it: the history, a supply shock, Schelling's limit and a planner's permits under hidden demand. This is a study of methods on a real place, not a housing policy proposal ([the record](../research/experiments/2026-11-05-households-of-the-centre/README.md)).
