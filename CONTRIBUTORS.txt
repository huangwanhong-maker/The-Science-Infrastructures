2. HOW A CONTRIBUTION IS MADE
-----------------------------

2.1 Inbound = outbound. A contribution is licensed under the same MIT
    License as the repository, to everyone, when it is submitted. There
    is no contributor licence agreement, and no agreement with the
    Society.

2.2 Each commit carries a sign-off (git commit -s):

        Signed-off-by: Full Name <address>

    by which its author makes the Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1,
    reproduced in section 7: that they have the right to submit the
    work under this licence.

2.3 Name the source of anything you did not write yourself and keep
    its notices. Do not commit material whose licence is incompatible
    with MIT: copyleft code (the GPL and its kin) or material under a
    non-commercial licence. Datasets are fetched into data/cache/ by
    the data library and are never committed.

2.4 The commits made before this file existed carry no sign-off. Their
    one author is the contributor first listed in section 6, who
    certifies by this file that section 7 holds for each of them.

2.5 A contributor is added to section 6 in the same change as their
    first contribution, under the name they choose. Anyone may ask to
    be listed differently or not at all; the history of the repository
    keeps what it keeps.

    docs/gallery.md section 10.
