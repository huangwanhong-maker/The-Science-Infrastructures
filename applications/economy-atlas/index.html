The engine and the data are not all made yet

The atlas computes with the economics domain's C++, compiled once here with g++ (natively, or in WSL), and draws the OECD's trade between economies, the ECB's exchange rates and the BIS's policy rates, fetched into the data cache. The recipe makes them in a few minutes.
